## Keep the action understandable

Use Tooltip for brief, nonessential descriptions. Keep names, instructions and
critical information visible without hovering. Content must contain no links,
buttons or other interactive elements. Use [Popover](/docs/popover/) for
interactive anchored content.

Trigger composes [Button](/docs/button/), including native props, guards,
`render` and refs. Forward all resolved props to one button. Tooltip owns an
`aria-describedby` relationship and merges existing description IDs; it does
not replace the accessible name. Content is a nonfocusable `span` with
`role="tooltip"`. Native disabled buttons cannot receive focus: place any
explanation in visible text rather than depending on their tooltip.

## Timing and dismissal

Keyboard focus opens immediately. Mouse and pen hover open after `openDelay`
(default 500 ms). Leaving both trigger and content closes after `closeDelay`
(default 150 ms); moving across the placement gap keeps the description open.
Both delays must be finite and nonnegative.

Escape dismisses the top overlay without moving focus. The current hover/focus
session stays dismissed; leaving and entering again permits reopening.
Clicking the trigger dismisses the tooltip while preserving its action. Touch
activation does not open it. No essential information should depend on touch
users discovering a tooltip.

Choose controlled `open`/`onOpenChange` or initial `defaultOpen`. Owners may
refuse state requests. Unmount cancels timers and listeners.

## Position and style

Content accepts `side`, `align`, `sideOffset` and `collisionPadding`, following
[Popover positioning](/docs/popover/#position-and-customize). Portal mounts
beside the trigger to preserve themes and modal ancestry; the top layer
escapes clipping. Keep the trigger mounted and visible.

Style Content through `className`, `style`, a span ref, `components.tooltip`
or local `--rad-tooltip-*` variables. Overrides include `maxWidth`, `padding`,
`radius`, `background`, `color`, borders, `shadow`, `fontSize` and `fontWeight`.
Preserve hidden closed content and readable wrapping. Optional popup motion
honors `motion="off"` and reduced motion.

Parts expose `tooltip-trigger` and `tooltip-content` slots plus `data-state`,
`data-side` and `data-align`. Root and Portal add no host. All part prop types,
`TooltipRenderProps` and `TooltipState` are exported; `useTooltip()` returns
state or `null` outside its owner.
