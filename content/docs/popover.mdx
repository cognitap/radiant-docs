---
title: Popover
description: Nonmodal anchored content that preserves the surrounding page.
---

```tsx
import {
  Popover,
  PopoverTrigger,
  PopoverPortal,
  PopoverContent,
  PopoverClose
} from '@cognitap/radiant'

;<Popover>
  <PopoverTrigger>Delivery details</PopoverTrigger>
  <PopoverPortal>
    <PopoverContent
      side="bottom"
      align="start"
    >
      <p>Standard delivery takes three to five working days.</p>
      <PopoverClose variant="outline">Got it</PopoverClose>
    </PopoverContent>
  </PopoverPortal>
</Popover>
```

## Own the content semantics

Popover supplies a nonmodal top-layer surface. Content stays a `div` without
an implicit role. Supply appropriate semantics and names: an interactive
preferences panel can use `role="dialog"` with `aria-label`. A menu or listbox
also needs its own keyboard and selection behavior; a role alone is insufficient.
Use [Dialog](/docs/dialog/) when the surrounding page must become inert.

Trigger owns `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; it adds no `aria-haspopup`.
Trigger and Close compose [Button](/docs/button/), including guards, native
form props, `render` and refs. Forward all resolved props to one button.
`preventDefault()` in their click handler cancels the state request.

## Focus and dismissal

Opening keeps focus on the trigger. Content's `initialFocusRef` can target a
descendant on each open. Native navigation places content after the trigger
in Tab order. Focus is never trapped. Moving focus outside dismisses it.

Escape dismisses the top Popover layer; Close dismisses its owner. They return
focus to the trigger when focus was inside. Outside pointer/focus dismissal
preserves the new target. Closing an ancestor Popover or Dialog hides its
descendants. Unmount removes observers, listeners and motion bindings.

Choose controlled `open`/`onOpenChange` or initial `defaultOpen`. Controlled
owners may refuse requests. Independent controlled surfaces may coexist.
Keep the trigger mounted and visible while using its content.

## Position and customize

Content accepts `side` (`top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`), `align` (`start`,
`center`, `end`), `sideOffset` (8) and `collisionPadding` (8). Numeric values
must be finite and nonnegative. Placement flips and shifts within the visual
viewport and updates after scrolling, resizing and content changes. Top/bottom
start/end follow RTL; left/right are physical sides.

Root and Portal add no wrapper. Portal mounts beside the trigger, preserving
themes, native forms and modal ancestry while the top layer escapes clipping.
Content owns its style/ref target. Use `components.popover` or local
`--rad-popover-*` variables, such as `width`, `padding`, `radius` and `shadow`.
Retain bounded scrolling, visible focus and hidden closed content.

Content exposes `data-state`, effective `data-side`/`data-align`, and
`data-slot="popover-content"`. Buttons add `popover-trigger` or `popover-close`
to their Button identity. Optional MotionProvider entry feedback honors
Content's `motion="off"` and reduced motion; closing stays immediate.

Each part's prop type, `PopoverRenderProps`, `PopoverSide`, `PopoverAlign` and
`PopoverState` are exported. `usePopover()` returns state or `null` outside its owner.
