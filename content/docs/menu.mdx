## Offer commands

Menu supplies a single-level command menu. Use [Link](/docs/link/) for
navigation and [Select](/docs/select/) for choosing a form value. Submenus,
menubars, checkbox/radio items and context-menu triggers are outside this API.
Keep Content limited to MenuItems; do not nest unrelated interactive controls.

Trigger composes [Button](/docs/button/), owns its ID and sets
`aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Content is a
`div` with `role="menu"`, named by the trigger. Give the trigger a meaningful
accessible name.

Items are Button hosts with protected `role="menuitem"`. `disabled` maps to
`aria-disabled`: commands remain discoverable through arrows but cannot
activate. `pending` retains Button's busy presentation and activation guard.
Native form props, refs and `render` remain available. Forward all resolved
props to one button.

## Navigate and activate

Enter, Space or Down on Trigger opens and focuses the first item; Up opens
the last. Within Content, Up/Down wrap, Home/End jump, and printable characters
search labels. Repeating a character cycles matches. Supply `textValue` for
rich labels; matching resets after 700 ms. Disabled items remain focusable.

Enter, Space or click runs `onClick`, then `onSelect`, then requests close.
`preventDefault()` cancels subsequent work and dismissal. Guards run before
consumer handlers. Native form submission remains the button's default action.

Escape dismisses the top overlay and returns focus to Trigger. Tab and
Shift+Tab close and continue from Trigger through the surrounding page.
Outside pointer/focus dismissal preserves the new target. Focus is never trapped.

Choose controlled `open`/`onOpenChange` or initial `defaultOpen`. Owners may
refuse requests. `useMenu()` returns state or `null` outside Menu. All part
prop types, `MenuState` and `MenuRenderProps` are exported.

## Position and customize

Content and Portal compose [Popover](/docs/popover/): local theme/modal
ancestry, collision-aware positioning, nested dismissal, cleanup and optional
popup motion travel together. Root and Portal add no wrapper.

Use Content's `className`, `style` and div ref or `components.menu` overrides:
`width`, `padding`, `radius`, surface colors/borders/shadow, `itemPadding`,
`itemRadius`, `itemFocusBackground` and `itemFocusColor`. Local variables use
`--rad-menu-*`. Preserve closed hiding, scrolling and visible focus.

Composed parts retain Button/Popover identities and add `menu-trigger`,
`menu-content` and `menu-item`. Content retains Popover's state, placement and
motion attributes. Reduced-motion and forced-colors preferences remain supported.
